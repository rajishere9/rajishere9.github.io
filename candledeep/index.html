Candledeep

A pixel-art survivor roguelite for Android, by rkjdev.

Contact support

Email rajankrishnan909@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and, for purchase issues, the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA.).

Purchases

  • Missing a purchase? Open the game, go to Settings > Restore purchases. Items you paid for (Remove Ads, Gold Doubler, Starter Pack) come back on any device signed in to the same Google account.
  • Payment still pending? Some payment methods take a while. The item is granted automatically once Google Play confirms the payment, the next time you open the game.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play: request a refund on Google Play.

Ads

Rewarded ads are optional. An ad may appear between runs, never during one. Buying Remove Ads removes all ads and makes ad rewards free. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can change your ad consent at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options.

Your data and deleting it

Candledeep has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress is saved only on your phone. To delete it, use Settings > Reset progress or uninstall the game. Data collected by Google's ads SDK is handled under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Privacy > Ads. To ask us about any data, email the address above. Details are in the privacy policy.
