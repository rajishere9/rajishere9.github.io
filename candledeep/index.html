CandleDeep

A pixel-art survivor roguelite for Android, by rkj dev.

About the game

Beneath every grave lies the Hollow. You wake at its gate with one candle of time. Survive the endless horde until the candle burns out, carry your gold back up, and descend again stronger. CandleDeep is played with one hand in portrait: move with the joystick while your weapons fire on their own, and choose an upgrade at every level-up.

  • Free, coming soon to Google Play (Android 7.0 and newer). Plays fully offline.
  • 2 stages, 20 enemies, 8 weapons with evolutions, 7 heroes, 23 achievements.
  • Rated ESRB Everyone 10+ / PEGI 7.

Purchases

  • Missing a purchase? Open the game, go to Settings > Restore purchases. Items you paid for (Remove Ads, Gold Doubler, Starter Pack) come back on any device signed in to the same Google account.
  • Payment still pending? Some payment methods take a while. The item is granted automatically once Google Play confirms the payment, the next time you open the game.
  • Refunds and cancellations: see the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Ads

Rewarded ads are optional. An ad may appear between runs, never during one. Buying Remove Ads removes all ads and makes ad rewards free. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can change your ad consent at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options.

Your data and deleting it

CandleDeep has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress is saved only on your phone. To delete it, use Settings > Reset progress or uninstall the game. Data collected by Google's ads SDK is handled under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Privacy > Ads. To ask us about any data, email the address above.
